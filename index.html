Welcome, Traffic Director

The city is counting on you. Your job is to control the intersections and keep the traffic flowing without causing a gridlock.

  • Traffic Lights: Click directly on any intersection to cycle the lights (NS Green -> Yellow -> EW Green -> Yellow).
  • Gridlock: If cars sit still for too long, your Gridlock Risk goes up. If it hits 100%, you lose.
  • Lane Laws: Cars are now strictly bound to their lanes. They will never cross the center lines while turning.
  • Upgrades: Spend your earnings to upgrade speed, traffic flow, and unlock city laws.
